Climate

A vegan diet has about a quarter of the footprint

In a study of 55,504 people in the UK, vegan diets caused about 25% of the greenhouse gas emissions and land use of high meat-eaters' diets (100 g or more of meat a day), and less than half the water use.

Scarborough et al., Nature Food, 2023
Animal welfare

About 1 in 5 dairy cows is lame

A 2023 review of 53 studies covering about 415,000 cows found that on average 22.8% of dairy cows were lame. Studies of UK herds found between 17% and 37%. Milk and cheese come from these herds.

Thomsen et al., The Veterinary Journal, 2023
Health

Processed meat increases the risk of bowel cancer

The WHO's cancer agency classes processed meat, such as bacon, ham and sausages, as carcinogenic, because there is sufficient evidence that it increases the risk of bowel (colorectal) cancer. Eating 50 g a day, about one sausage or two rashers of bacon, increases the risk by about 18%. Red meat is classed as probably carcinogenic.

IARC Monographs Q&A, Volume 114
Eating plant-based well

Plan for a few nutrients

The NHS says a varied vegan diet can give you everything you need. Plan for vitamin B12 (fortified foods or a supplement), plus iodine, calcium, iron and omega-3.
